The Masslak mark and wordmark: a route between two stops on a green rounded square.

Use `withName` for the lockup in app bars, drawers and auth screens; the bare mark for favicons, avatars and tight spaces. `gold` switches the route line to `brand-gold` for marketing and stores.

- Props: `size` (px, default 36), `withName`, `lang` ("ar" or "en"), `tagline`, `gold`.
- Do: keep clear space of one third of the mark; place the mark on the reading-start side of the word.
- Don't: recolour the square, add shadows, or set the wordmark in another family.
