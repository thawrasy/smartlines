The Masslak mark: one arc from a light-blue origin stop (#7FB0FF) rising in blue (#2F7BFF) to a green destination stop (#12A06A), on a navy rounded square (#0B1F3F). `masslak-mark.svg` is the default; `masslak-mark-outline.svg` is a white square with a navy edge, a #0A5BD3 route and a #2F7BFF origin; `masslak-symbol.svg` is the route alone in #0A5BD3 for white grounds; `masslak-symbol-white.svg` is the route in white with the green stop, for navy grounds. `app-icon-ios.svg` is the full-bleed 1024px iOS master; `app-icon-android-foreground.svg` is the adaptive foreground for a #0B1F3F background layer. The wordmark is typeset (see README).
